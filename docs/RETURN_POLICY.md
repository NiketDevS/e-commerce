# RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

## Section 1: Overview

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, you may request a return or refund within the eligible return window, subject to the conditions below.

**Keywords:** overview, return, refund, satisfaction, policy

---

## Section 2: Return Window

You may request a return within **7 calendar days** of the delivery date shown in your order.

After 7 days from delivery, the order is no longer eligible for a return or refund.

The delivery date is the date recorded in our system when the order was marked as delivered.

**Keywords:** return window, 7 days, days, delivery date, eligible, deadline, expired

---

## Section 3: Eligible Product Categories

The following product categories are eligible for return and refund:

- **Electronics** — eligible within 7 days. Item must be unopened or defective.
- **Clothing and Apparel** — eligible within 7 days. Items must be unworn with original tags.
- **Home and Kitchen** — eligible within 7 days. Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- **Books and Stationery** — eligible within 7 days. Books must be in resalable condition.
- **Sports and Fitness** — eligible within 7 days. Equipment must be unused.
- **Toys and Games** — eligible within 7 days. Must be in original sealed packaging.
- **Beauty and Personal Care** — eligible within 7 days. Must be unopened and unused.

**Keywords:** eligible, categories, electronics, clothing, apparel, home, kitchen, books, sports, toys, beauty, personal care

---

## Section 4: Non-Eligible Product Categories

The following categories are **not eligible** for return or refund:

- **Digital Downloads and Software** — non-refundable once accessed.
- **Perishable Goods** — non-refundable (food, fresh produce).
- **Customized or Personalized Items** — non-refundable unless defective.
- **Hazardous Materials** — non-returnable.
- **Intimate Apparel and Swimwear** — non-returnable for hygiene reasons.

**Keywords:** non-eligible, not eligible, non-refundable, digital, software, perishable, food, customized, personalized, hazardous, intimate

---

## Section 5: Refund Amount

The refund amount is calculated based on:

- The original purchase price of the item(s) being returned.
- Minus any previously refunded amount for the same order.
- Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.

Partial refunds may be issued for partially returned orders.

**Keywords:** refund amount, amount, price, partial, shipping, calculation

---

## Section 6: Refund Thresholds and Human Review

- Refunds of **₹5,000 or less** that meet all eligibility conditions are processed automatically and typically credited within 5–7 business days.
- Refunds **exceeding ₹5,000** require review by our support team. You will be notified within 2 business days of the decision.

**Keywords:** threshold, 5000, human review, manual review, automatic, processing time, approval

---

## Section 7: Refund Methods

Refunds are credited to the original payment method:

- **Credit/Debit Card** — 5–7 business days.
- **UPI / Net Banking** — 3–5 business days.
- **Store Credit** — applied within 24 hours (if requested).

**Keywords:** refund method, credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, store credit, timeline, credited

---

## Section 8: Defective or Incorrect Items

If you received a defective, damaged, or incorrect item:

- Contact support within **48 hours** of delivery.
- We will arrange a free replacement or full refund, including shipping charges.
- Please provide your order number and a description of the issue.

**Keywords:** defective, damaged, incorrect, wrong item, replacement, 48 hours, complaint

---

## Section 9: Return Process

To initiate a return:

1. Submit a support ticket through your account dashboard.
2. Describe the issue and the item(s) you wish to return.
3. Our AI agent will check your eligibility based on your order details.
4. If eligible, the refund will be processed automatically (for amounts ≤ ₹5,000) or reviewed by our team.
5. You will receive confirmation through the ticket conversation and by email.

**Keywords:** how to return, process, initiate, support ticket, steps, dashboard

---

## Section 10: Cancellation Policy

- Orders can be cancelled within **1 hour** of placement if they have not yet been shipped.
- Once shipped, the order must be returned after delivery — it cannot be cancelled in transit.

**Keywords:** cancel, cancellation, cancel order, 1 hour, shipped

## Section 11: Contact Support

If you have any questions about your return or refund:

- Use the **Support Chat** in your account dashboard for immediate assistance.
- Our AI agent is available 24/7 to check eligibility and initiate returns.
- Complex cases are escalated to our human support team.

**Keywords:** contact, support, chat, help, questions, human, escalation, assistance
